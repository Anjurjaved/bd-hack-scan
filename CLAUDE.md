## ⚠️ Platform reality (2026-07-15) — account is Cloudflare FREE plan
`[limits] cpu_ms` is REJECTED (code 100328). The FREE-plan CPU wall (`error 1102` "exceeded resource limits") is the load-bearing constraint: a heavy `scanDomain` (regex over ~600KB HTML) 1102s a shard, which used to leave a poison domain pinned at the queue head = permanent stall. Two defenses are now in code and MUST be preserved:
- **Optimistic pre-mark (deadlock-breaker):** `scanSlice` (and `/vm-pull`) advance `pass_no` for the whole slice BEFORE scanning. `ingestResults` NO LONGER increments pass_no (only sets the `dead` flag) — never re-add it or every row double-counts.
- **Two-phase `scanFanout` + per-slice/per-domain budgets + 96KB `readCapped`** keep every invocation inside the free envelope; dashboard "last tick" reads `counters.last_tick_ts` (real scan) not cf-engine `last_seen`.
- **The real fix = Oracle VM** (see below) — CF free can't be made fast.
